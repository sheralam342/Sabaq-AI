📖 Sabaq AI - Your Personalized AI Textbook Tutor
Sabaq AI is an intelligent study companion designed specifically for Pakistani students in Matric (9th/10th) and Intermediate (11th/12th). It transforms static textbook PDFs into an interactive learning experience by generating practice tests and providing multilingual feedback.

Streamlit App OpenAI Python

🌟 The Problem
Students in Pakistan often struggle with vast textbooks and a lack of personalized practice. While they have the content, they often don't know if their answers meet the board exam standards or struggle to understand complex concepts in pure English.

🚀 The Solution: Sabaq AI
Sabaq AI bridges this gap by allowing students to upload their own textbook chapters. The AI then:

Analyzes the specific content of that chapter.
Generates board-style MCQs and Short Questions.
Evaluates the student's answers based strictly on the provided textbook text.
Explains mistakes in the student's preferred language: English, Urdu (اردو), or Roman Urdu.
✨ Key Features
📄 PDF Integration: Upload any chapter PDF for instant analysis.
📝 Smart Question Generation: Creates customized practice tests (MCQs & Short Questions).
✅ Context-Aware Grading: Checks answers against the textbook to prevent AI "hallucinations."
🌍 Multilingual Support: Get explanations in:
English: For formal academic learning.
Urdu: For deep conceptual understanding.
Roman Urdu: For ease of reading and digital communication.
🎓 Level-Specific: Tailors the tone and difficulty for either Matric or Intermediate students.
🛠️ Tech Stack
Frontend: Streamlit (For a fast, responsive web UI).
LLM (Brain): OpenAI GPT-4o-mini (For high-accuracy text analysis and translation).
PDF Processing: PyPDF2 (To extract text from textbook files).
Environment Management: python-dotenv (To securely handle API keys).
